"How long will the permit take?" is usually the second question an owner asks, right after cost. The honest answer has two parts: the time to prepare drawings the building department can approve, and the time the department takes to review them. The studio controls the first part. The jurisdiction controls the second. This article walks through both, using the steps on the [process page](/process/), for homes and additions in the Orange County area.

## First, check who reviews your permit

Orange County reviews permits for unincorporated areas. Many addresses with an "Orlando" mailing address are actually inside a city, and cities such as Orlando, Winter Park, Winter Garden and Apopka run their own building departments. Each one has its own submittal format, its own review queue and its own fees. Confirm which one covers your parcel before anything else. ## The steps, and how long each one usually takes

These are the typical durations at Maneda Design Studios. Your project may run shorter or longer, depending on its size and on how quickly decisions are made.

### 1. Inquiry and scope

You send the inquiry form. You get a reply within 1 business day and a scoping call within 3 business days.

### 2. Proposal and agreement

A written proposal with scope, deliverables and fee follows within 5 business days of the call. The schedule starts when the agreement is signed.

### 3. Site and code review: 1 to 3 weeks

The lot, zoning, setbacks, flood zone and the jurisdiction's rules are reviewed before design starts. This is where you learn whether you need a new survey or soils tests. A site problem found here costs days. Found at permit review, it costs weeks.

### 4. Concept and schematic design: 4 to 6 weeks

Usually three options, with sketches, plans, elevations and massing studies. You choose one.

### 5. Design development and revisions: 4 to 6 weeks

The chosen design is resolved: room sizes, openings, materials and finishes.

### 6. Construction documents: 8 to 12 weeks

The approved design is developed into the drawing set the building department reviews and the builder prices from. Structural drawings are prepared at the same time by a structural engineer, hired by the owner under a separate contract. The permit set is not complete until the structural drawings are in.

### 7. Permitting: 3 to 8 weeks

The set is submitted and reviewed. Reviewers may issue comments, which are answered and resubmitted. MDS supports this step, including responses to review comments. The 3 to 8 weeks is a typical range across jurisdictions, not a promise. The building department sets its own review time.

Added together, a new home or a large addition designed from scratch usually takes 17 to 27 weeks to reach a permit set, then 3 to 8 weeks of review.

## What slows a permit down

Most delays come from the drawings, not the reviewer. The usual causes:

- **Incomplete information.** Missing dimensions, details or notes lead to comments and a second review.
- **An old or missing survey.** Setbacks and elevations need a current survey to check against.
- **Flood zone questions.** A lot in a flood zone has rules on floor elevation and construction. Sort them out during site review, not at the counter.
- **Late structural drawings.** The architectural and structural sheets must agree. If the engineer starts late, the whole set waits.
- **Changes after submittal.** A design change during review usually means revised sheets and more review time.
- **Other approvals.** An HOA or a historic district may need to approve the design before or alongside the permit. [VERIFY: whether Orange County requires HOA approval before issuing a permit.]

## How to shorten the timeline

- **Start with the site review.** Constraints found early do not delay the permit later.
- **Hire the structural engineer early**, so the structural drawings run alongside construction documents.
- **Make design decisions on time.** Most schedule slips come from reopening decisions already made.
- **Order the survey early** if you do not have a current one.
- **Answer review comments quickly.** The review clock usually restarts with each resubmittal.

If your design is already decided, the work can start at construction documents, which saves the design phases. The [full-service design page](/services/full-design/) describes construction documents and permit support in detail.
