A clear process saves time on both sides. This is how a project runs with Maneda Design Studios.

## 1. Inquiry and scope

You send the inquiry form. The reply comes within 1 business day and a scoping call within 3 business days. You get a clear read on fit and the next step.

## 2. Proposal and agreement

A written proposal arrives within 5 business days of the call. It lists the scope, the deliverables, and a fixed fee set by phase. The schedule starts when the agreement is signed.

## 3. Site and code review

This takes 1 to 3 weeks. The studio researches the jurisdiction: which code applies, the size and height limits, and the seal and engineering triggers. You receive a constraints summary that frames the design.

## 4. Concept and schematic design

This takes 4 to 6 weeks. You receive concept sketches, massing and plan studies, 3D views, and a schematic design set.

## 5. Design development and revisions

This takes 4 to 6 weeks. You receive a design development set and material and finish direction. Two rounds of revisions are included in each phase.

## 6. Construction documents and permit support

Construction documents take 8 to 12 weeks, then permitting takes 3 to 8 weeks, depending on the jurisdiction. You receive a construction document set and support through permit review comments.

## How payment works

Each phase fee is invoiced in two parts: half to start the phase and half on delivery. Invoices are due within 7 days, and the next phase starts when the invoice is paid.
