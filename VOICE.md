# Voice & Positioning

Source: Bio.docx (Guia de Bio & Posicionamento). Archetype: Sage (dominant) + Hero (secondary).

## Core Positioning

The bio is not a resume. It is a point of view. It answers the question every client asks unconsciously before any other: why does this designer understand my project?

The central asset: an architectural designer with 10+ years of professional practice who understands a project from program and site through construction documents and permitting. Every piece of written content orbits this fact.

## Bio Structure: Three Fixed Moves

Order is fixed, never inverted.

1. **Value proposition**: what the client gets, not the designer's name first
2. **Credential**: the architectural design background, stated as context, not vanity
3. **Call to action**: direct, no pressure. The Sage does not beg, he invites.

## Bio Versions by Context

**Instagram (max 150 characters):**
Architectural design from concept to construction documents. Designer for 10+ years. Portfolio below.
No hashtags, no decorative emojis. Arrow points to the link, not the feed.

**Short bio (site / LinkedIn):**
Three sentences, three moves, no wasted words. Value proposition → credential → invitation to view the portfolio.

**Medium bio (about page / proposal):**
Can be split into three standalone paragraphs. Each paragraph works alone. Closes on: drawings that do not only document a project. They resolve it.

**Long bio (press kit / full about page):**
Opens with the founding question. Middle section on the design career. Closes by naming the audience directly: homeowners, architects, developers, and real estate professionals who want their projects designed and documented with the same seriousness they are conceived with.

## Outreach Tone by Segment

**To architects and builders:** Collegiate, not salesy. Speak as an equal. Lead with shared design and technical vocabulary, not a pitch.

**To homeowners:** Clear and plain. Explain the process and the phases. Define terms when needed. Never talk down.

**To real estate / developers:** Professional, value-oriented. Focus on what the client gains: schedule, coordination, a permit-ready set. Not what the studio does.

## Voice Rules

The Sage does not self-promote. He demonstrates. Every sentence should read as if written by someone who has more to say than they're saying, and chose to say only the essential.

**Avoid:** passionate about design, dream home, stunning, award-winning, one-stop shop, DM me, special prices, years of experience, dedicated professional.

**Use:** design intent, program, site, massing, circulation, materiality, coordinate, resolve, document, deliver, from concept through construction documents.

No exclamation points. No excessive emojis. No direct sales language. No em or en dashes.

## Manifesto

*Architecture is intention made space. Design is the discipline of making that intention buildable.*

Usable on: homepage, proposal header, first slide of any presentation.
